# Brand palette reference

## Alpine Survey — original website treatment

This is the palette applied to the website before the technical-consulting refinement on 2 September 2026.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Alpine Ink | `#102A33` |
| Accent | Glacier Blue | `#2E6F89` |
| Secondary accent | Survey Gold | `#C38A32` |
| Background | Snow | `#F4F7F5` |

Supporting colours were `#E8F0F2` for pale blue surfaces, `#17313A` for body text, `#596C73` for muted text, `#CBD7DA` for rules and borders, and `#9BC7D7` for light accents on Alpine Ink.

In the original treatment, Survey Gold appeared on the top border of every expertise card and on the example-list bullets. Snow was also used for the page and header background. The favicon used Alpine Ink, Survey Gold and Snow.

To restore it, set Snow back to `#F4F7F5`, use `rgba(244, 247, 245, 0.96)` for the header, and restore Survey Gold on the expertise-card top borders and example bullets. The complete original state is retained in GitHub commit `aeb003713de847410c99a76b3becc0fe5cb8b982`.

## Current technical-consulting treatment

The same identity is retained, but Snow is cooled to `#F5F7F7`, Glacier Blue carries functional and structural accents, and Survey Gold is reserved for the logo detail.
